# Privacy Notice — Denver Bulldogs "Bulldogs OS"

> **STATUS: DRAFT — for club committee approval.** This notice is not live until the
> committee names a data controller and approves this wording. Until then, the site
> only runs on synthetic test data. (Per `plan.md` §9 and §12.)

**Who we are.** The Denver Bulldogs Australian Rules Football Club ("the club") runs
Bulldogs OS, a small web app for player recruitment and club operations. The club is
the **data controller** for the personal information described below. A named
committee member holds the data-controller role — record their name and email here
once approved: _TBD — committee to nominate._

## What we collect

When you use the "Come try footy" sign-up form, we collect:

- **First name** (required), last name (optional)
- **Email address** and **phone number** (required, so a volunteer can reach you)
- **Your consent** to be contacted by a club volunteer about playing
- **Your answers** to optional questions (preferred first session, experience,
  pathway, how you heard about us)
- **Activity on your record**: enquiries, assigned volunteer, call attempts, notes,
  training attendance and milestones — recorded by club volunteers as you progress

We do **not** collect dates of birth, medical information, payment details, or
anything you don't type into the form. Anonymous visitors are only counted in
aggregate analytics.

## Why we collect it

- To have a **club volunteer contact you about playing** for the Denver Bulldogs —
  that is the only reason we ask for your contact details.
- To run the club: assign leads to volunteers, track training attendance, keep
  season records, and report participation numbers to the committee.

## Who can see it

Your contact details and notes are visible only to **signed-in club staff with a
role** (admin, coordinator, volunteer, coach, calendar editor), each limited to what
their role needs. Volunteers see the leads assigned to them. Prospect contact
details are never shown publicly.

## How we contact you

- After you sign up, you receive **one automatic confirmation email** with practical
  first-training information.
- After that, any contact is made **by a person**, not automation. In V1 there are no
  automated drip campaigns, SMS, WhatsApp messages, or reminders to prospects.

## Where it is stored and how long we keep it

- Data is stored in our backend provider **InsForge**, project `denver-bulldogs`,
  hosted in the **US West** region, with encryption in transit and role-based access.
- We keep your record while you are an active prospect or player, and afterwards for
  club history (seasons, attendance, results). You can ask for erasure at any time
  (below); we may keep a minimal record where the club has a legitimate need (e.g.
  audit trail of who changed a record), but we will remove contact details.

## Your rights: export and erasure

- **Export:** all club data, including your record, can be exported to **CSV** on
  request — the club owns its data and can take it anywhere.
- **Erasure / correction:** email the data controller (address TBD above) and we will
  delete or correct your personal information, and tell you when it's done.

## What we never do

- We **never sell or share** your personal information with third parties.
- We do **not** add you to WhatsApp groups or messaging lists automatically.
- We do **not** send your private details to AI/LLM services; the only AI workflow in
  V1 works on aggregate, non-identifiable counts.

## Minors

V1 is designed for **adult self-signup**. We do not knowingly collect information
from minors; if a junior player is involved, a parent/guardian must contact the club
directly so a separate approved process can be set up.
